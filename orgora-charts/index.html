Orgora Charts — Support

Orgora Charts lets you build and manage org charts privately on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. All data stays on your device.

Download on the App Store · Install Orgora Clipper for Chrome or Safari · Open Orgora

Open Orgora does not open a chart on this site yet. It waits until a chart can save to your own Google Drive or OneDrive.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
The phone and Mac keep the chart on the device. You can also save that chart to your own Google Drive or OneDrive. That file is the chart, not a receipt, and it is not stored on an Orgora server.

How does the same chart open on the phone and the web?
Sign in with the same Google or Microsoft account. The web writes the chart file. The phone opens that same file. You can have both open. If the saved file is newer than the copy on this device, the app asks you to keep this device or use the other copy.

If I pay, am I charged twice?
No. Pro is recognized on the phone and the web for the same account, so you are not charged twice. Cancel the one you paid and Pro ends on both.

How do I move data to a new device?
Restore the new device from a backup of the old one, and your charts come with it.

Is email sync required?
No. It is optional and off by default. If enabled, the app talks directly to your own email provider and stores credentials only in your device keychain.

Can I clip LinkedIn profiles from Google Chrome?
Yes. Safari Clipper is already inside Orgora Charts. For Chrome on a Mac, add the free Orgora Clipper extension — you do not write any code. Install Clipper.
