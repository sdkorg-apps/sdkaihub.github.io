Orgora Charts — Support

Orgora Charts lets you build and manage org charts privately on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. All data stays on your device.

Download on the App Store · Install Orgora Clipper for Chrome or Safari

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
Entirely on your device. There are no accounts and no servers. Your data is included in your normal device backups.

How do I move data to a new device?
Restore the new device from a backup of the old one, and your charts come with it.

Is email sync required?
No. It is optional and off by default. If enabled, the app talks directly to your own email provider and stores credentials only in your device keychain.

Can I clip LinkedIn profiles from Google Chrome?
Yes. Safari Clipper is already inside Orgora Charts. For Chrome on a Mac, add the free Orgora Clipper extension — you do not write any code. Install Clipper.
