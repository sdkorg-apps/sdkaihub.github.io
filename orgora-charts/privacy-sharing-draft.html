Sharing charts

You can share a chart as a read-only .orgora file. Share files are created entirely on your device and are sent only through channels you choose (AirDrop, Messages, Mail, iCloud Drive, or any app in the share sheet). They never touch SDK AI Hub servers — we have none — and we have no way of knowing that a share happened, who received it, or what it contained.

A share file may contain personal data about the people on your chart (such as names, job titles, and work email addresses — and, only if you explicitly switch it on, your private notes and activity data). By default the app excludes notes, lead status, email activity, and phone numbers from share files. You are responsible for having the right to share the information in a chart, and for choosing recipients you trust: anyone who has the file can read it and pass it on.

Charts shared with you are stored locally on your device, like everything else. Sharing changes nothing about our headline promise: your data never leaves your device unless you send it to someone. The App Store privacy label remains "Data Not Collected."
